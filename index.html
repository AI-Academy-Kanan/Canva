Task 2 · 15 marks

Build in Canva: Template to Your Deck

Search “Presentation” in Canva, pick a 16:9 template, and turn it into your 5-slide deck. A template is a starting point, not the finished deck: replace the text, add relevant images, use your colours and keep only what's needed.

1. Pick your topic

2. Your deck must have

  • 4 to 7 slides
  • 2 fonts only: heading + body
  • 3 colours: main, support, accent
  • Title the biggest text on every slide
  • Titles ≥32 pt, body text ≥24 pt
  • The same title size on every slide
  • Relevant images on at least 2 slides
  • No leftover template text

3. Export and upload
